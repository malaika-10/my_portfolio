# Standalone Project 03

/project03/ presents the four slides of portfolio shama.pdf in their original
order and proportions. Each has a 1537px responsive and 3074px HD graphic with
screen-reader text. The page is unlinked from navigation and galleries and has
noindex/nofollow metadata. It remains publicly accessible to anyone with its URL.

## Detail-page interaction policy

All six detail pages (the linked Androon Rawalpindi, Shama and Vanishing Stories
pages, plus Project01, Project02 and Project03) display slides without clickable
image wrappers, zoom cursors or viewer dialogs. Obsolete viewer scripts have
been removed. Existing animated glasses, driver animations, video playback,
looping and audio controls remain. Homepage and All Projects gallery cards still
link to their respective project pages.

This removes the zoom interaction; it does not prevent screenshots or browser
access to the image assets.
