# Standalone Project 01

/project01/ is an unlisted seven-slide presentation from the supplied Drive PDF
14atR7gHAAhOZVK6F6KqnL17qTq35UL87. It is not linked from any public navigation or
project gallery. A noindex/nofollow directive discourages search indexing; the
page is still publicly accessible to anyone with its URL.

Each slide has 1537px responsive and 3074px HD WebP graphics at its original
aspect ratio. Original media objects were removed from the rendering copy before
adding centered ±10°/20-second glasses motion on slide 2, the three transparent
animated drivers on slide 6, and the existing VR intro and rickshaw videos in their
respective regions on slide 7. Videos autoplay muted while visible, loop, have
native controls without a download-menu option, and include independent sound
buttons. Enlarged slide views preserve the animations and playable media.

The PDF source is unchanged. Existing project pages and galleries are unchanged.
