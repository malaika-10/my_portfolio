SHHH… THE STORIES ARE DISAPPEARING! What is Vanishing stories? What happens to a story when the last person who remembers it is no longer here to tell it? Vanishing Stories is a magical digital home for stories at risk of being forgotten. Kids can discover hidden tales, preserve memories, and share their own, because every story deserves to live on.

ONCE UPON A MEMORY… Some stories are never printed. They live in a grandmother’s voice, in a poem recited before bedtime, in a funny family incident or in a tale that changes slightly every time it is told. These stories may feel unforgettable, but oral memories are fragile. When they are not recorded or retold, they can slowly disappear. How might we preserve oral childhood memories without removing the warmth, personality and human voice that made them special? The platform is designed for both children and adults. Children experience a playful story world, while parents and grandparents become contributors to a growing cultural archive. Vanishing Stories asks: Target Audience:

Existing projects also demonstrate that ordinary voices can become important historical records. StoryCorps reports that more than 750,000 people have participated in its conversations, with recordings collected in its online archive and the U.S. Library of Congress. About StoryCorps However, preservation involves more than pressing a record button. Ethical oral-history practice requires respect for narrators, informed participation, cultural sensitivity and awareness of power between the person recording and the person sharing. Oral History Association Principles and Best Practices WHY SHOULD WE CARE ? Oral traditions include stories, poems, songs, myths, riddles and other knowledge communicated through speech. UNESCO identifies oral traditions and expressions as an important part of intangible cultural heritage. Because they depend on people continuing to practise and transmit them, language loss, migration and social change can place them at risk. UNESCO: Oral Traditions and Expressions

MY ROLE I shaped Vanishing Stories from an initial idea into a complete digital experience, combining interaction design, storytelling and playful visuals to preserve disappearing childhood memories. I designed the app’s interface, user flow and interactive screens, making it simple, engaging and easy for children to explore. I researched, collected and adapted traditional stories and poems into digital formats while preserving their original emotions and cultural value. I animated the characters, story elements and interface transitions to make the app feel lively and responsive. These animations guide children through the experience while making storytelling more immersive and enjoyable. APP & EXPERIENCE DESIGN STORIES & POEMS ANIMATION & INTERACTIONS
